# Raw catalogue inputs

Immutable acquired bytes belong here locally and are ignored by default. Do not add
catalogue bytes until source, access, licence, retention, and redistribution decisions
are approved.

The Milestone 2D.1B Gaia TAP responses are retained under the ignored path recorded by
`data/manifests/gaia-dr3-hip-screen-v1/acquisition-manifest.v1.json`. The tracked
manifest identifies every local byte by length and SHA-256; it does not authorize
committing or deploying those rows.

# Arabian sky claim review checklist

- [ ] One atomic name, identification, membership, segment, or route claim is stated.
- [ ] The claim is classified as `OLD_ARABIAN`, `NAJDI_TRADITION`,
  `GRECO_ARABIC_SCHOLARLY`, `CROSS_REGIONAL_ARABIAN`, or `MODERN_PEDAGOGICAL`.
- [ ] Exact Arabic, transliteration system/output, and translation are recorded.
- [ ] Source, verified edition, page/verse/folio, period, and geographical scope are
  explicit.
- [ ] OCR/text-layer and edition limitations are recorded.
- [ ] Modern catalogue-ID identification has its own evidence and uncertainty.
- [ ] Coordinates remain outside cultural records.
- [ ] Alternate readings, names, identifications, and source conflicts are visible.
- [ ] Historical evidence and modern instructional construction are not conflated.
- [ ] Evidence status and human-review status are separate.
- [ ] `NAJDI_TRADITION` has explicit Najdi evidence and human review.
- [ ] No Arabic term is presumed ancient Arabian, Bedouin, or Najdi merely because it
  is Arabic.
- [ ] Unresolved claims are excluded from learner-facing content.
